# FutureLens AI – Predict Before You Decide

FutureLens AI is a predictive decision intelligence platform that helps users evaluate risks before making real-world decisions such as travel planning, investment choices, and event attendance.

## Problem

People often make decisions with incomplete information, which can lead to safety risks, financial losses, or unexpected outcomes.

## Solution

FutureLens AI analyzes multiple real-time data sources such as:

- Weather conditions
- Traffic data
- News sentiment
- Financial market trends

The AI system then generates:

- Risk scores
- Explanations
- Safer alternative suggestions

## Example

Input:
"Trip to Ooty tomorrow"

Output:

Risk Score: Medium

Reasons:
- High rainfall probability
- Fog visibility issues

Suggested Action:
Travel early morning or delay the trip.
